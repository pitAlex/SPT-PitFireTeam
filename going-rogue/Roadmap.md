# Going Rogue roadmap

Current encounter, standing, recruitment and hostile-task rules are in [Rogue alliance](docs/Rogue-Alliance.md). Quest content is in [Knight quests](docs/Knight-Quests.md); build/deployment evidence is in [validation](Validation.md).

Implemented Rogue combat and its Knight → Big Pipe → Rifleman hierarchy are described in [exUsec combat](docs/ExUsec-Combat.md). Live squad-navigation and command qualification remain in the validation ledger.

## Future quests

The seven current tasks end with Tripwire and its permanent Birdeye unlock. Any extension of the chain must deliberately update the recruitment milestone schedule and automatic companion admission for unfinished objectives. No future quest IDs are admitted before their content exists.

## Future hostile-task discovery

The existing eight legacy Rogue/Goon-kill tasks have been verified against installed SPT 4.1.6. Future versions/custom tasks need objective-aware conflict discovery or an audited extension of that list, including status handling, invitation/preparation/spawn gates and friendship suspension.
