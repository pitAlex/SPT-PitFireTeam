# Rogue alliance

Core owns [Allegiance encounters](../../docs/Allegiance.md), recruitment conversion, roster storage and the standard [combat tactics](../../docs/Combat-Tactics.md). Going Rogue extends those contracts through optional recruitment and companion admission policies. Core has no typed dependency on this addon.

## Progression and recruitment

The player is an ally. Existing PMC/Scav roster members keep their profiles and roles. Deploying with a Goon prevents friendly PMC encounter selection. Rogue-only accompaniment instead adds one 15% relative reduction to PMC encounter chances, regardless of Rogue count. This temporary squad modifier adds to actual kill penalties without creating a saved kill entry. Scav chances are unaffected by accompaniment.

Handing in Competition unlocks exUsec friendship, including grouped Rogues and raids without the Goons. Rogue friendship is independent of the ambient solo-PMC/Scav population cap and recruitment acceptance. Recruitment requires native Success on all seven Knight quests and standing of at least 1.0. Ready-to-hand-in, failed or missing quests do not satisfy that gate. Without a Goon escort, base acceptance is 50% at standing 1.0, increasing linearly to 70% at 2.0 and remaining capped at 70% above that. Existing timed recruitment penalties reduce this unescorted chance. Levels, Fence reputation and donation bonuses do not replace it.

A living, active Knight, Big Pipe or Birdeye registered to this human guarantees Rogue acceptance after those quest and standing gates. Ambient, foreign, dead, inactive or merely selected Goons do not qualify. The guarantee bypasses chance penalties and can supersede an earlier sampled refusal without another random roll. Ordinary sampled answers remain stable. Stationary guard duty, hostile-task/betrayal suspension, pickup limits, combat refusal and ownership/conversion checks still apply. After successful field recruitment, the normal surviving-recruit invitation flow does not impose another level/chance roll and independently requires all seven quests.

Rogue recruitment and deployment require Knight standing >=1.0. Invited Big Pipe and Birdeye require >=0.5, while unlocked Knight has no additional standing minimum. Unlocks and availability are separate: low standing never deletes an existing member. The server rechecks invitation, auto-join, saved-kit preparation and actual spawn. Temporary quest companions retain quest-specific admission rather than the saved-member standing threshold, but all obey the hostile-task restriction.

After Competition Success, allied Rogue groups exclude the local player from native warning-zone intruder targets. This works without invited Goons and independently of recruitment standing. Suspended alliances, other gameplay modes and non-Rogue warning groups retain their native behavior. Other intruders are not filtered.

Rogues with a taken stationary weapon, an assigned weapon they are moving to take, or a stationary weapon in their hands refuse recruitment with Negative. This check precedes acceptance rolls and is repeated before deferred conversion. Friendly interaction cannot pull a guard away from his job.

Post-raid capture admits native Savage-side exUsec followers only when the addon owns their combat role. Ordinary Scavs remain excluded from permanent-roster invitations and keep their separate cargo return path. The captured Rogue role and kit reach the existing server companion policy; already accepted saved members cannot create a new recruit request. The ordinary server flow still selects at most one invitation from the eligible surviving candidates per submission.

Saved Rogue teammates retain ordinary gear swap and body/container/loose-loot commands. Knight/Pipe movement leadership does not apply the Goons' fixed-equipment or body/container command restrictions to Rogues. A freshly field-recruited Rogue still uses the ordinary recruit Give Gear and loot-fallback rules until accepted into the roster.

## Hostile quests

Hostile tasks are detected from native quest objectives rather than a fixed ID list. Only completion (`AvailableForFinish`) kill conditions explicitly targeting exUsec, Knight, Big Pipe or Birdeye suspend the alliance. Failure conditions, prerequisites, hit-only objectives and unrestricted Scav/PMC kills do not qualify. Mixed named-role kill objectives that include a Rogue/Goon do qualify. The client reads bound native templates, including custom/repeatable quests; the server resolves current registered templates and profile-local active repeatable definitions at admission, after other mods have registered their content.

Current quest status is read at every existing friendship/recruitment/deployment check, with no persistent conflict cache or extra polling. Startup template binding, acceptance, hand-in, failure and replacement therefore take effect on the next check without restarting. Started and AvailableForFinish suspend Rogue friendship and block saved Goons/Rogues from joining, even when their kill counters are already complete. Client quest-companion previews and server temporary preparation/spawn also refuse automatic Goons. Completed/failed tasks release the restriction; cached legacy friendship cannot override it. A missing template supplies no inferred kill target; the server independently rechecks admission against its definitions. This does not dismiss teammates already spawned or clear native combat enemies.

Declined roster invitations retain the native group-decline notification. Knight, Big Pipe, Birdeye and exUsec members also reply in their own user-chat conversation when the refusal is caused by a hostile task or low Knight standing. The addon maps each restriction to a centralized localized reply key; Core owns delivery and sender identity. Other invitation failures keep their existing system messages. Personal replies use native mail with sender metadata and preserve existing conversations, including repeated declines. The delayed notification and reply share the existing mode-generation gate, so switching modes suppresses both pending messages together. No message changes the restriction, standing or quest state.

Started/AvailableForFinish hostile tasks also explicitly admit the human and living squad as enemies of ambient Rogues and Goons through native `BotsGroup.AddEnemy`, rather than merely removing alliance protection. This matters for USEC players because native Rogues otherwise use warning behavior. The existing addon update observes the current GameWorld before evaluating this rule, without requiring a betrayal kill or any followers. Late spawns/members are included; owned companions remain player allies. Native admission failures are retried without bypassing validation, rewriting shared settings, assigning goals or granting sight/fire permission. Successful new ambient-to-human admissions log their actor, role and hostile-task/betrayal reason. Inactive/completed/failed tasks do not create new task hostility; existing living enemy memory is retained.

## Standing and raid hostility

After Competition Success, a player kill of an ambient or field-recruited exUsec costs 0.025 Knight standing; an ambient Knight, Pipe or Birdeye costs 0.1. Saved teammates deployed with the player and temporary quest Goons are exempt from these taxes and the alliance betrayal flag, just as saved PMCs are exempt from encounter penalties. The server also rejects stale standing/encounter reports targeting saved members. The legacy standing floor is 0.1. Ambient standing penalties still apply during hostile quests. AI kills do not tax the player. The addon captures victim role before native death cleanup and deduplicates by raid/victim. A queued HTTP worker serializes reports without blocking combat and uses native player-profile receipts committed alongside standing; duplicate/retried reports cannot deduct twice. Native save confirmation is required before acknowledgement. Existing quest and raid-end standing rewards retain their owner.

Killing a friendly ambient or field-recruited Rogue also adds the standard 15% relative PMC/Scav encounter reduction and a separate 15% relative reduction to Rogue recruitment acceptance for 24 real hours. Both use the same tagged kill entry and expiry. Multiple actual kills add their reductions, capped at 100%; ordinary recruit kills reduce encounters only. First-hit friendship is captured before health processing or native retaliation, so a lethal first shot is included and already-hostile victims are excluded from these two friendship penalties. Existing sampled answers remain sticky; unrolled Rogue recruitment requests use the current penalty. At seven completed quests, one active friendly-Rogue kill reduces 70% acceptance to 59.5%. Betrayal prevents further recruitment for the current raid; the timed acceptance reduction carries into later raids until expiry.

Either non-exempt kill sets a raid-local hostility flag. All living ambient exUsecs and Goons become enemies of the player and the player's living teammates; later spawns and later recruited teammates are included by the addon update. Native AddEnemy owns the relationship changes; no goal, sight or firing permission is granted. Recruited/invited Goons and Rogue members remain player allies and are never themselves converted into enemies of the player. A new GameWorld resets hostility; standing remains saved.

## Combat extension

Allied Goons add their current enemy to friendly ambient exUsec groups within 150 metres using `BotsGroup.AddEnemy(enemy, EBotEnemyCause.addCauseGroup)`. The receiving group must be friendly to the human player; owned teammates are excluded from ambient recipients. Native enemy admission and the Rogues' existing AI decide the response.

Going Rogue owns the [exUsec teammate layer](ExUsec-Combat.md): Knight commands first, Big Pipe when Knight is absent, and ordinary Rifleman decisions without either. This does not change ordinary hostile Rogue brains, friendship or recruitment admission. Core retains its ordinary ExUsec factory outside the addon mode.

Source: shared `RogueAlliancePolicy`, client/server `RogueAlliance`, server `RogueStandingService`, and Core's optional `GameplayModeAddons`/`FriendlyModeCompanion` policies. Dated checks and deployment belong in [validation](../Validation.md).
