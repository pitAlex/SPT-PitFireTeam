## Ownership and fallback

Going Rogue registers `ExUsecCombatLayer` for living, active, registered exUsec teammates with an ExUsec brain and the local human owner. It reuses the Goon layer lifecycle, reversible bot-local native/external-SAIN lease, Core combat driver and shared actions. Native ExUsec request/group-attack/stationary layers are additionally suspended for these owned actors; danger and maintenance retain their existing owners. Saved spawning waits for this layer's readiness. Ambient/hostile Rogues and other modes retain their existing brains. Runtime tactic is fixed to Rifleman before follower initialization and proficiency capture.

Before addon readiness, Core preserves marked teammates' native exUsec role through SPT brain activation and safely declines the native strategy constructor's stationary-weapon lookup when its cover/voxel connection is not initialized. This prevents a native activation failure at affected player spawn points without changing role, extending registration timeouts or affecting ambient Rogues. See [Core activation compatibility](../../docs/Architecture.md).

`ExUsecCombatObjective` runs Core's actual Rifleman selector once. Core offers optional companion planning only after urgent fighting, weapon/medical work, recovery, existing movement/cover/position/push commitments and ordered routing. Optional advancement permission restricts independent advances. A separate admitted commander-advance callback makes the selected commander's live same-target advance authoritative over the Rogue's aggression score and personal-sighting requirement. It still requires an eligible known shared contact and preserves survival, weapon, complete-path and movement/fire-safety gates. The callback publishes no decisions. Ordinary Rifleman construction retains its original two-argument constructor and null callbacks.

## Command hierarchy

The leader is a living, active Knight belonging to the same human; otherwise a living, active Big Pipe belonging to that human; otherwise none. Birdeye is not elected. Presence alone does not invent an order. Knight's native managed-member positions remain restricted to Pipe/Birdeye: native allocation has only two slots. `RoguePositionCoordinator` supplies Rogue positions for either commander and shares only already-admitted leader contacts. Pipe's selected combat decisions publish advance/search/covering-fire intent; exUsecs use that existing intent when Pipe leads. An idle, healing, pressured or differently engaged selected commander cannot be replaced by a lower-priority command source.

The allocator counts available same-human Rogues and proposes bounded sectors behind/lateral to their commander. Each exact point uses Core's existing firing-lane, backline, alternate-threat, floor and complete-path admission, plus physical ground support. Actual sampled destinations must remain at least 6m apart horizontally on nearby levels, including the human, allies, native Goon assignments, active movement destinations and other Rogue reservations. Each member may probe six points, with at most twelve firing-point probes across the allocator per quarter second. The best valid result is reserved before movement publication; a single good point produces a single assignment. Rejected points never fill a quota. Failed plans leave ordinary Rifleman/support behavior available.

Stable assignments remain reserved after arrival, and are reconsidered when the commander changes, moves over 8m, the enemy report moves over 10m, the target changes or a point fails revalidation. Dead/removed members release their reservations. Short follow and commander push-reassignment destinations must satisfy the same spacing gate; a failed prepared reassignment releases its unused reservation. Successful new assignments emit passive `exUsec.command / positionAssigned` diagnostics.

Follow steps retain admitted-search tracking until their normal action/lifecycle release. Movement and arrival remain Core-owned. Rifleman engagement distance and pull use the selected commander as well as the movement anchor. Rogues retain positional cohesion with that commander even at 100% determination; independence removes human pull rather than unit cohesion. Player Regroup and pending commands retain the human anchor. Recorder arbitration fields include `anchorProfileId`, `currentAnchorDistance` and `projectedAnchorDistance`; older player-named distance fields remain aliases for compatibility.

Rogue automatic pushes and commander-follow steps recheck advance permission every 0.25 seconds, matching Pipe's leadership response. When permission is withdrawn, a concrete one-second reevaluation hold must be prepared before the Rifleman owner clears its push/movement commitments and stale prepared advance. Failed preparation preserves those commitments. Useful fire, medical work, reload/draw/grenade work, player orders, incoming pressure and unsupported/airborne footing retain priority. Dogfight actions are excluded. This does not cancel an explicit player push merely because automatic advance permission changes.

Player push admission and later commander reassignment share Pipe's command implementation. The selected commander's confidence verdict is authoritative unless Pipe's existing nearby-player delegation applies: the member is within 30m of the player and the commander is over 50m away, fighting another fresh target, or lacks that contact. With neither commander, ordinary Rifleman push admission applies. Need Help, Regroup and other non-push commands are not superseded by commander reassignment. Protected medical/weapon/grenade work and useful current fire also prevent reassignment. Only a materially changed Pipe intent creates a new directive; repeated publication is not a new command.

## Peaceful following

Peaceful patrol follows that same hierarchy: Knight, then Pipe, then the human player. It depends on who actually spawned and remains living/active, rather than assuming the full Goon squad. Holding the current commander keeps his uncommanded members nearby. Player commands still take priority for their recipients. Birdeye is never elected, and ordinary PMC followers do not join this movement hierarchy.

## Settings

The addon supplies `LockCombatSettings` for exUsec profiles. Profile UI hides tactic, aggression and Self-Determination controls; proficiency, clothing, identity and ordinary equipment controls remain available. The server rejects tactic/aggression edits and ignores the determination field on proficiency requests, so direct requests cannot bypass the lock. The owned Rogue runtime aggression baseline is 60%, rising to 68% with living active Knight, including existing recruits. Determination is 100% with Knight, otherwise the member's saved value, including under Pipe alone. Pipe retains the higher 70% Knight-unit aggression. Explicit player percentage overrides retain priority. Removal, mode exit and teardown release the baseline without rewriting saved preferences. Stored tactic selections resolve to Rifleman for deployment rather than selecting another brain. No per-raid equipment generation or Goon loot restrictions are added.

Native Unity navigation, simultaneous multi-Rogue fights, recruitment conversion and in-game profile layout require live qualification; controlled checks do not establish those outcomes.
