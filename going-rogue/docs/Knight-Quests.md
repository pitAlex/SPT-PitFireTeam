# Knight quests

Core's [quest assist](../../docs/Quest-Assist.md) also admits Goons deployed from the roster for ordinary quest kills within 80 metres. Generic roster forwarding is excluded from the Knight-owned counters; their existing dedicated Goon death handler remains the single owner, preserving personal-kill and role/proximity conditions without double credit. Temporary quest companions do not receive general quest assist.

Core base: [architecture](../../docs/Architecture.md), [localization](../../docs/Localization.md) and [Knight trader](Knight-Trader.md). Current relationship/recruitment rules are in [Rogue alliance](Rogue-Alliance.md).

Going Rogue extends Allegiance and registers its first seven quests through SPT's native `CustomQuestService`. The addon embeds the definitions and EN/RU/GE/RO locales, with English fallback for other locales. No Virtual's Custom Quest Loader, Virtual's Custom Quests content pack or WTT dependency is required. Native EFT/SPT owns quest status, counter persistence, item handover, restart handling, rewards, notifications and prerequisite delays. Quest 3 chooses one of three configured Streets sports bags and saves the selection per player and quest attempt.

The first quest uses `/files/quest/icon/KnightCompetitionQuest.jpg`. My Land uses `/files/quest/icon/KnightMyLandQuest.jpg`, the enhanced Customs stronghold screenshot with dark muted cinematic grading and softly blackened edges matching Off Limits, with dialogue describing occupying the base rather than reclaiming an existing home. They robbed us uses the original `/files/quest/icon/KnightThieves.jpg`; Badge Bandit uses the original `/files/quest/icon/KnightRobocop.jpg`. Enemy Spotted uses the original `/files/quest/icon/KnightBirdEye.jpg`. Off Limits uses `/files/quest/icon/KnightCoverMe.jpg`, updated from the supplied Shoreline Pier screenshot with the marked corpse location behind the water tank cleaned up, the player PMC walking, and a dark desaturated cinematic grade with softly blackened edges. The revised seventh quest uses `/files/quest/icon/KnightPartizan.jpg`, reframed from the supplied Partizan poster to 314×177, prioritizing his head and upper torso. ## Who let the dogs out?

- ID: `6775d9957e2dbcb3bd0a02c7`; available at player level 20.
- Eliminate five targets on each of Woods, Shoreline and Lighthouse, with a living allied Knight within 80 metres. Only the human player's kills count.
- The player and Knight must survive. Knight's death on one of those maps fails the active/ready task. The legacy native exit conditions also fail KIA, Left and MIA on those maps.
- Rewards: 32,500 XP, +0.1 Knight standing and permanent Knight roster access after hand-in. Restartable.

The description separates Knight dialogue and player requirements into paragraphs: Knight's original competition dialogue, then plain player-facing requirements in third person. The requirements state five personal kills per map, automatic Knight deployment when entering an objective map alone as a PMC with the quest active, and the 80-metre distance at each kill. It explicitly rules out leaving Knight in one place while hunting elsewhere and states that either the player or Knight dying in a shared quest raid fails the task. It also states that dying in a raid without Knight does not fail it. It is not phrased as Knight asking the player to keep him alive. EN/RU/GE/RO descriptions explain that automatic joining requires an unfinished objective on the selected map; description, note and survival objective all scope the failure rule to Knight-accompanied quest-map raids. The descriptions separate the blocks with `\n\n`; native `NotesTaskDescription` assigns the localized description directly to `TMP_Text.text`, preserving the blank line. The note retains the actual kill/proximity/survival requirements; objective rows keep their original concise wording.

The completion message keeps the original Knight dialogue and appends a separate player-facing paragraph after one blank line: Knight is now in the roster and can be invited to raids; continuing the quest line unlocks the remaining Goons and Rogue recruitment. EN/RU/GE/RO use the same paragraph structure.

The three kill objective rows retain the original wording: "Eliminate 5 targets on [map] while teamed up with Knight". Personal-kill/proximity details belong in the separate requirements paragraph and note. Maintained translations use the correct map for each objective and targets rather than introducing a PMC-only condition.

The old counter patch admitted any Goon's presence for the personal-kill objectives, despite their text requiring Knight. The new admission requires Knight specifically. Ordinary teammate, Big Pipe and Birdeye kills do not count.

Quest 1's native `Success` status unlocks Knight in Allegiance. The normal roster/social read creates him once through Core's existing companion creation path, so completions from earlier builds are repaired without replaying the quest or changing its progress/rewards. Started and AvailableForFinish do not qualify. Existing Knight identity, skills, health, equipment and Proficiency are retained. The unlock receipt is separate from retired test-seeding receipts and is committed atomically with a newly created profile; removing an already-granted member does not repeat the grant. Other modes are unaffected. Big Pipe's equivalent grant requires native Success on Badge Bandit (`67768936fa281ca31708b17c`) and Knight standing of at least 0.5 when granted. Birdeye permanently joins the roster after native Success on the Partizan hunt (`67768a41fa281ca31708b182`), without an additional standing threshold. Started and AvailableForFinish do not qualify; existing successful completions are reconciled automatically with a separate atomic receipt. Existing saved Birdeye members are retained. Unlocking roster membership does not force raid-group selection; the old `/singleplayer/autoteam` path separately returned configured automatic invitations.

## My Land

- ID: `6775da597e2dbcb3bd0a02c9`; level 20 and successful completion of the first quest, followed by the legacy 3,600-second delay.
- Eliminate eight targets in Customs Scav Base, using the native victim-zone condition `prapor_27_1`, with a living allied Knight within 80 metres. The human player and Knight can contribute; other teammates cannot.
- Knight's death on Customs fails the active/ready task. The legacy native player exit conditions also fail KIA, Left and MIA on Customs.
- Rewards: 24,000 XP, +0.1 Knight standing and $2,000. Restartable.

Neither task needs custom zone creation. The legacy text mentioned Ran Through failures, but neither JSON exit condition included that status; the port preserves those actual conditions and corrects the text. Obsolete parenthetical promises about legacy chat invitation unlocks are removed. Automatic test seeding of Knight, Big Pipe and Birdeye is disabled. Quest 1 grants Knight roster access after hand-in as described above. [Rogue alliance progression](Rogue-Alliance.md) grants friendship after quest 1; recruitment requires all seven successes and at least 1.0 Knight standing. An eligible living Goon escort guarantees acceptance; otherwise the standing-based chance rises from 50% at 1.0 to 70% at 2.0, before applicable relative recruitment penalties.

## They robbed us!

While Knight and the player were clearing their new home, the other Goons reported that Big Pipe's launcher had been stolen from the Lighthouse chalet. Men left to guard the gear were killed; the launcher alone was taken and everything else remained untouched. Knight concludes that the thief knew what they wanted and waited for an opportunity. His inquiries lead to the Hive hookah lounge on Streets of Tarkov; the dialogue leaves the person behind the theft unidentified for quest 4.

The description is two paragraphs of in-character dialogue only. Its straightforward retrieval objective does not need explicit mode, FIR, team-composition, distance or failure explanations in Knight's speech or an additional note. The objective rows use the old mod text verbatim in EN/RU/GE/RO. English: "Look for BigPipe's grenade launcher in the Hive hookah lounge, in Streets of Tarkov." and "Hand over the weapon." The native FIR requirements are enforced by the conditions; no FIR suffix is appended to the objective text.

- ID: `677687f2fa281ca31708b17a`; level 20 and My Land successfully completed, with no additional delay.
- Find and hand over one exact special launcher (`64e3b0f4e3b6f5a4a37e8c1d`) Found in Raid. The ordinary M32A1 cannot satisfy either native objective. Native ordinary inventory and item handover own possession and consumption.
- Streets of Tarkov retrieval, without a kill objective, companion proximity/spawn requirement or custom death-failure condition. Teammates may accompany the player. Allegiance with the Going Rogue addon remains mandatory.
- Rewards: 15,000 XP, +0.1 Knight standing and $2,000. Original restartable flag and no failure conditions retained.
- Native `CustomItemService` clones the current M32A1 definition with legacy ungivable/unbuyable flags, size and handbook price, preserving current game fields without modifying the ordinary weapon. The original five-item default preset and attachment IDs are registered before native preset initialization. The addon owns the new item through SPT's mod-item cache; ordinary PMC loot and flea listings do not gain this quest item. The special launcher has `QuestItem = false`: this flag selects ordinary inventory placement, allowing retrieval, carrying and equipping; it does not remove the exact-template/FIR quest conditions. `QuestItem = true` restricts items to the separate quest inventory and produces the native regular-container rejection seen during testing. Item and quest text live in the embedded addon locales.

**Launcher placement:** the pool contains `container_City_SE_02_DesignStuff_00005`, `container_City_SE_02_DesignStuff_00002` and `container_City_SE_02_DesignStuff_00004`. Successful acceptance chooses one existing container; already-active quests acquire their choice on their next eligible raid. The encrypted Allegiance database stores `knight-launcher-placement.json`, keyed by native quest start time. The choice survives retries, raids and restarts; a new attempt selects again. If a game update removes the saved ID, selection repairs from the remaining configured IDs. An entirely missing pool logs an error and leaves ordinary loot intact.

Placement runs on the session's generated Streets loot only, for PMC raids in Allegiance with the addon, the task Started and no FIR special launcher already in the player's inventory. It stops for other modes, Scav raids, other maps, skipped loot generation and completed tasks. The chosen bag is guaranteed even if native container randomisation omitted it. Native container generation packs the forced launcher before random contents; the selected bag is regenerated, while other bags and shared location tables remain unchanged. A zero-item roll is repaired using the registered preset and native inventory placement. Generated item identities are independent; repeated processing of the same result cannot add another launcher. Failures leave the original raid loot untouched. Selection and successful placement are logged as `[KnightQuestLoot]`.

The binding changes the next generated raid, not an already-loaded scene. Container IDs still require review after game updates; the three installed matches are validated in the server fixture. Source: `server/KnightQuestLootModule.cs`.

### Self-contained pier zone

The original `FriendlyPMC_Zones.json` is embedded byte-for-byte in the client addon. The supplied map coordinates remain valid. On `GameWorld.OnGameStarted`, an Allegiance PMC with an active/ready Off Limits task on Shoreline receives a native `QuestTrigger`/trigger `BoxCollider`, with ID `pier_zone01`, on EFT's Triggers layer. Other maps, modes, Scav raids and inactive tasks do not create it. Position is `(-324.9464, -62.07625, 520.8833)`; quaternion is `(3.694647E-08, 0.4226183, -7.923195E-08, -0.9063078)`; full dimensions are `(55, 17.75, 112)`. The collider is a centred unit box scaled by those dimensions, preserving the original rotated volume rather than approximating it with an axis-aligned box or radius.

EFT's native physics-trigger search and entry/exit callbacks maintain every player's `TriggerZones`, including AI victims. The existing native `InZone` condition evaluates those zone IDs; Goon kill forwarding captures the victim's zone list before death cleanup. There is no addon per-frame population scan, hand-written kill-coordinate approximation, solid collision geometry, map bundle edit or external quest-loader dependency.

Setup is idempotent, reuses an existing active trigger with the same ID without taking ownership, and does not let a disabled foreign trigger suppress the new area. An addon-created zone is parented to its GameWorld, disabled and destroyed on owner disposal or addon unload. Cleanup from another world cannot remove it. Other creators' zones are never destroyed. Source: `client/Quests/KnightQuestZones.cs` and embedded `client/Resources/quests/FriendlyPMC_Zones.json`.

### Prerequisite waits after installing new quests

`availableAfter` is always a whole-second unlock deadline: computed prerequisite completion plus delay is rounded up, so fractional native completion timestamps cannot shorten the wait or break EFT's integer JSON reader. Before native profile cloning or quest listing, the addon also rounds existing fractional `availableAfter` values on its seven quests, regardless of quest status or selected mode. This wire-format repair leaves quest status, start time, objectives, rewards and fractional `statusTimers` history unchanged. Creating or retiming prerequisite waits remains Allegiance-only; other traders' quests are untouched.

SPT's native first-acceptance path creates a fresh `AvailableAfter` wait when a delayed quest has no saved profile record, even if its prerequisite was completed hours earlier. That leaves the server task pending and prevents automatic companions/preview selection, which correctly requires Started. The addon backfills the native pending record before profile serialization, quest listing and acceptance, deriving its deadline from the prerequisite's saved Success timestamp and the unchanged definition's delay. Native existing-record acceptance then starts the task normally. This covers My Land, Badge Bandit, Enemy Spotted and Off Limits without adding a second wait on installation or acceptance.

For an already affected pending record, only its incorrect deadline is repaired. Started/ready/successful/failed quests, counters, completed objectives, prerequisite history and rewards are not reset; no quest is automatically started. Missing or invalid success timestamps are not fabricated. The repair is scoped to Allegiance and addon-owned delayed quests. After restarting into a corrected profile, an affected pending quest becomes available through native timing and must be accepted again; eligible solo-map previews then follow its Started status. Source: `server/KnightQuestWaits.cs` and `server/KnightQuestModule.cs`.

## Quest companion deployment

On a PMC raid with no selected group member, an enabled Knight trader and a Started task, Going Rogue requests the task's companions automatically. The addon admits Knight for the first task only on Woods, Shoreline and Lighthouse, and My Land only on Customs; Badge Bandit admits all three Goons on Streets, Enemy Spotted admits only Birdeye on Woods, and Off Limits admits Pipe/Birdeye on Shoreline. Core's selection path requires Allegiance with the addon and an empty group; other modes and raids with any selected teammate do not request quest companions. These checks are independent of the roster and do not require an invitation.

Automatic admission also requires unfinished work on the selected map. Competition maps to its native Woods, Shoreline and Lighthouse objective IDs independently: completing Woods prevents later automatic Knight deployments there while leaving unfinished Shoreline/Lighthouse deployments available. My Land similarly checks its Customs objective. Both the client selector (confirmation, loading, launch and raid role selection) and the server (preparation and actual spawn) read the current quest's native `CompletedConditions`; no progress is copied, inferred from another map or reset. Completing an objective during a raid does not dismiss the Knight already accompanying the player. A future implemented quest can still request its own needed companion on the same map.

The server separately checks the active mode's registered companion policy, PMC raid configuration, selected-member count, quest status/map, level 20 and enabled trader. It generates the native role and reuses the normal boss reserve/magazine provisioning without saving a teammate or default loadout. Knight joins through the boss-ally path with `squadMate: false`; Core waits for both registration and addon combat readiness. Fresh Going Rogue bosses retain their generated health instead of receiving the legacy extra head/limb multipliers. Existing saved-party preparation and transit reuse remain in place.

Eligible automatic Goons also appear in Prepare to Escape and the loading party panel without an invitation. Core uses the same quest/map/solo selector for preview and raid selection, and the existing preparation endpoint reserves each entire temporary native profile, keyed by role under one loading token. Account identities are distinct across those reservations. Actual spawn rechecks eligibility and consumes the role's reservation, preserving the displayed identity and equipment; stale tokens, changed map/time, mode generation or missing reservations cannot silently reroll it. A lost response retains strict tokens for every requested role. Visual entries exist only in the native preview controller, never the saved roster or selected-member list, so they cannot defeat solo admission. Inviting a teammate removes all of them and releases every automatic reservation. Closing preparation detaches the visual entries; loading rebuilds them from the same reservations. Transit continues using carried profiles rather than generating fresh quest kits. The prior singular profile request/response remains supported.

The temporary visual supplies the same native matchmaking nickname/lock metadata as ordinary teammate previews. EFT formats `SavageNickname` even on PMC confirmation screens before binding the model, so this field must be non-null; it falls back from the current player's nickname to Knight's nickname and then an empty string. This affects preview metadata only, not the reserved native profile or raid equipment.

Core registers the group preview reopened on local confirmation screens with the native screen UI lifetime, so Back disposes its models as well as removing the temporary group entry. An incoming teammate invitation clears the automatic visual and cached quest profile/token even when confirmation is already closed; it does not generate equipment against the previous map. On reopening confirmation, the selected map and current invited group determine eligibility again. Moving to another eligible objective map still qualifies; a non-quest map or any invited member does not. Native group callbacks cannot promote the automatic preview into a selected teammate.

Companion confirmation normalizes both the displayed and stored offline `RaidSettings` copies to Local. Native Ready clears `IsPveOffline` and may apply the stored copy; updating only the displayed copy can restore Online when the preview makes the party count two. Core rechecks current quest/map/party admission at `TarkovApplication.OnReadyToStartMatchingAsync`, before EFT selects local versus network matching, and sets Local/`IsPveOffline` there. This does not depend on the temporary visual surviving screen close or on a cached quest profile. Invited teammates retain the same shared local-launch protection. Native `LocalGameCreate` still sends raid settings through the existing spawn-selection hook; Core does not manually start a second raid or bypass native startup.

For the initial test, accept Who let the dogs out, leave the group empty and enter Woods, Shoreline or Lighthouse. Human kills with a living Knight within 80 metres should advance that map's native counter. After extracting, invite a regular teammate and enter another raid: quest Knight is absent and the custom kill counters do not advance. A raid with no participating Knight also cannot trigger either task's player-exit failure counter, even if the player dies. Knight participation is scoped to the current GameWorld and cannot carry into a later raid without him; after Knight participated, follower cleanup cannot erase that participation to evade player-death failure. Switching modes preserves saved progress while disabling quest listing/acceptance/completion, auto-spawn and custom counter updates.

## Admission and ownership

The addon filters its quests from native quest listings outside Allegiance without deleting saved profile progress. Accept/complete/item-handover requests reuse Knight's server mode, level-20 and native disabled/access checks. In-raid custom counters are inactive outside Allegiance, including failure counters. The third task uses native item possession/handover rather than the companion kill/failure counters.

The native static `ConditionCounterManager.Test(int, TaskConditionCounter, ConditionCheck[])` prefix admits only the local player's quest instances. Existing Core teammate-credit calls temporarily expose the actual killer profile; personal and team objectives gate those identities separately. Unrelated counters are unchanged.

Goons are allied recruited members without Core squadmate quest-credit privileges. The addon records successful local follower registration for the current GameWorld, tracking Knight, Pipe, Birdeye and any-Goon participation separately, then captures kill/death identity before native follower cleanup. The participation markers let native exit failures work after cleanup without affecting later unaccompanied raids; raid selection and world changes reset them. Knight kills can submit native kill/location/victim-zone checks to My Land, any Goon can submit the same checks to Badge Bandit, Birdeye can submit PMC/Woods checks to Enemy Spotted, allied Goons can submit Any/InZone checks to Off Limits while the required pair is present, and Birdeye can submit Savage/Partizan/Lighthouse checks for the seventh task. Inputs use the target tag required by each native counter: Any for My Land, Savage for Kollontay and AnyPmc for Enemy Spotted. A scoped counter-ID gate prevents synthetic credit from reaching any other counter. The addon never replaces the quest controller's profile or changes general teammate quest credit. Victim callbacks are deduplicated per GameWorld. Companion deaths use native quest status transitions, including restartable failure, and are captured before cleanup can lose ownership.

Source: `server/KnightQuestModule.cs`, `shared/KnightQuestPolicy.cs`, `client/Quests/KnightQuestRuntime.cs` and `server/Resources/quests/` in this addon. Fixtures/build/installed metadata evidence is recorded in [validation](../Validation.md). Live quest UI, zone credit, extraction/failure/restart and reward delivery require EFT qualification.
