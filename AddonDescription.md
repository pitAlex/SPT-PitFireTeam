## Description

The **pitFireTeam SAIN Addon** adds two combat tactics for players who want SAIN-style fighting in their player-led squad: **SAINGrunt** and **SAINShooter**.

It brings SAIN's individual and squad combat into pitFireTeam while keeping you in charge. Teammates use cover, react to danger, support one another, and respond to your orders instead of behaving like an independent AI squad with its own leader.

**Requires pitFireTeam, SAIN 4.5.1, and BigBrain.** Use an addon build that matches your pitFireTeam release. Installing SAIN alone does not unlock these tactics.

**Choose your role:**

- **SAINGrunt** — an all-purpose fighter for the Rifleman role. He supports the squad, suppresses enemies, and advances when his equipment and the fight allow it. Use **Go Forward** to pressure a known enemy, or **Regroup** to call him back.
- **SAINShooter** — an alternative to Marksman, focused on ranged support, firing positions, and useful sightlines rather than assault pushes. Use **Need Sniper** to ask him to find a firing opportunity.

To use either tactic, open **My Squad → Roster**, view a teammate's profile, and select **SAINGrunt** or **SAINShooter**. You can mix them with Rifleman and Marksman teammates in the same squad. Recruited bots using SAIN can also join with SAINGrunt when the addon is available.

**Your existing squad controls still apply:**

- **Aggression** controls willingness to pressure enemies and pursue a fight.
- **Self-Determination** controls how closely combat behavior stays tied to you. Lower values favor escort and support; higher values give the teammate more independence.
- **Vision, Precision, and Reaction** still tune each teammate's performance.
- **Hold Position**, **On Your Own**, **Cover Me**, **Go Go Go**, and **Attention** retain their shared combat-setting effects.
- **There**, **Come To Me**, **Regroup**, and **Exit Located** let you direct positioning, while **Status Report** keeps you informed.

**Suppress** asks suitable teammates to put short pressure on a known enemy position. They can also support a nearby teammate's fight or advance when they have an opportunity. Healing, reloads, grenades, and immediate danger still take priority.

**Preparing for heard threats:** followers may pause, face nearby hostile footsteps or voices, and move into defensive cover before seeing the enemy. These pauses can be preparation rather than a refusal to follow you; hearing someone does not give them sight through walls.

Outside combat, following and peaceful commands continue through pitFireTeam. My Squad, recruitment, progression, looting, Swap Gear, Give Gear, insurance, and item returns keep the main mod's rules. This addon changes how the selected teammates fight—not who owns their equipment or what they return after a raid.

## Limits & Differences

- **Not identical to Rifleman and Marksman.** The roles have similar purposes, but use SAIN's combat behavior. Expect differences in cover choices, movement, pursuit, firing positions, and when a teammate returns toward you. This is an alternative combat style, not a promise that every fight will go better.
- **More hit-and-run fighting.** SAIN favors taking cover more often than the main mod's Rifleman and Marksman. This is especially noticeable at the start of a fight, when followers may quickly break away and run for cover, leaving you exposed in the open. Be ready to find your own cover rather than expecting them to stay beside you.
- **Higher Self-Determination is recommended.** SAIN's combat is designed around each bot making its own fighting decisions. SAINGrunt and SAINShooter generally perform better with more independence, allowing them to follow through on those decisions instead of being repeatedly pulled back toward your position. Lower values favor closer escort, but can work against SAIN's natural combat style.
- **Your SAIN preset matters.** The same Aggression percentage can feel different between presets. The addon draws its fighting style from your loaded SAIN personalities: **50%** uses Normal as its reference, **70%** Chad, and **100%** GigaChad. Higher Aggression does not directly mean better aim; use Proficiency to adjust shooting performance.
- **No grenade-launcher suppression.** The addon's Suppress command uses suitable firearms, not grenade launchers. Hand-grenade throwing is separate and still follows pitFireTeam's grenade setting and safety checks.
- **SAINShooter rarely uses his secondary weapon.** While he is meant to be an alternative to Marksman, an important current limitation is that he rarely, if ever, switches to his secondary. Do not rely on him changing weapons for close combat.
