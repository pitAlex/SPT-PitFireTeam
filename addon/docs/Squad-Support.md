# SAIN squad suppression and ally support

**Core base:** [combat tactics](../../docs/Combat-Tactics.md) and [commands](../../docs/Commands.md). Core owns command targeting and eligibility. This adaptation follows its suppression lifetime, survival priority and prepared-support principle using SAIN 4.5.1 execution. It does not change Core combat.

## Ownership

`SAINFollowerSquadSupportObjective` retains the target, destination and lifetime. The existing publication filter selects Squad `Suppress` or `Help`; `SAINFollowerSquadSupportAction` executes the owned intent. Unowned native squad decisions retain their existing actions. No solo-layer action or native provider is added. The native decision manager remains the single publisher.

The addon-only `SainSquadSupportBridge` hooks Core's existing suppression-issue boundary for ready addon followers. It preserves Core targeting/selection and limits Grunt orders to the current capable firearm and Shooter orders to Core-eligible automatic support weapons. Cached delegates reuse Core's weapon, soft-obstruction and push-support positioning predicates and SAIN's private exact-target aiming method. Installation validates these bindings and rolls back the addon hook set on failure. Core/unready followers and ordinary SAIN bots retain their existing behavior.

## Ordered suppression

- Both tactics consume the accepted Core `SuppressEnemy` command once. An explicit target identity wins over the current goal; the receiver must already know that enemy through SAIN. Missing native knowledge can wait up to three seconds inside the original pending command lifetime, then declines without substituting another enemy.
- Current-weapon eligibility follows Core's automatic-fire or magazine-capacity predicate. Grenade launchers are excluded. Ready SAINShooter retains Core's player-facing/no-Rifleman candidate rules and eligible automatic secondary/holster selection. The addon removes only the prior addon exclusion in that Core command method. Shooter preparation first allows up to three seconds for the selector to settle, then accepts one Core weapon switch and waits up to three further seconds for readiness before the six-second execution budget starts; timeout cancels, and pending preparation cannot move or fire. See [support weapons](SAINShooter.md#automatic-support-weapons).
- Visible support fire tolerates `CanShoot` flicker while the enemy remains visible: an already-firing burst may continue for at most 0.5 seconds from its last verified native shot, matching Core cover-fire grace. It retains that actual aim point and firing spot; every continuation checks Core's direct-geometry predicate, target and muzzle friendly lanes, native weapon readiness and actual muzzle alignment within 18 degrees. Moving more than 0.75 m, running, losing safety, pausing or expiry ends owned continuation. It neither restarts an ended burst nor renews the timer from a failed sensor sample. This is an adaptation of Core's bounded continuity; hidden-target suppression retains its separate admission and lifetime below.
- A visible shootable target uses SAIN's exact-target aim/shoot pipeline. Hidden suppression first tries the target's native `SuppressionTarget`. If absent or its lane is rejected, the addon binds Core's existing `FollowerSuppressTargetPolicy.TryGetTarget` for a same-identity, non-visible EFT contact: personal/squad reports qualify only within two seconds, each position paired with its own report timestamp. The fallback never enters Core's live body-position branch when native sight is absent, creates contact, or refreshes native memory.
- Both point sources use Core's look mask, shared friendly-lane safety and the hard-versus-foliage obstruction classifier immediately before firing. Native points retain `SuppressPosition`. Fresh-report fallback instead owns a shot through public `ManualShoot.TryShoot` with friendly checking enabled: native suppression ownership is released once on entry, so its updater cannot reset the shot or replace the admitted report with a missing/rejected native point. The addon mirrors native enemy-suppressed status while firing and clears its manual shot/status on interruption, completion or source handoff. Source changes retain the original burst deadline. Global suppression enablement, zombie exclusion, ammunition/running rules and native alignment/weapon/friendly/trigger safety remain authoritative. A turn or trigger delay retains that point instead of overwriting steering with a different native look point in the same tick.
- The burst lasts up to two seconds after actual firing begins; aiming alone does not start it. The attempt is bounded to six seconds. When no usable suppression lane exists, SAIN's firing-position finder may supply one committed destination, with a two-second arrival opportunity. This action does not use native suppression's continuous enemy pursuit.
- New commands, lost/dead contact, combat release and survival work stop the attempt and release owned firing, path and claim state. A different immediate visible threat temporarily retains native combat priority. A cancelled/completed action stays inert until the next normal publication replaces it, rather than falling into stale native suppression.

## Automatic ally support

Both addon tactics may leave a passive cover hold to support the player or a teammate. Ordinary ally admission requires at least one second settled in usable cover, no fresh personal fight, no incoming pressure/critical health or pending treatment, and no active order, push, regroup or committed movement. Grunt also considers nearby-push support and boss protection at a passive native cover boundary, without waiting for the ordinary ally hold timer. Initial cover selection, cover shifts and compromised-cover recovery retain priority. A usable shot or committed destination must be prepared **before** the existing hold is released.

Support cues provide identity only: the receiver must have its own valid native known contact. Hidden live coordinates from another actor are not used. Shooter retains its existing player-engagement / nearby push / ally-fight selection. Grunt gives boss-under-attack protection and nearby push support priority over ordinary player engagement and teammate fire.

### SAINGrunt boss and push support

- Boss protection follows Core Rifleman's `IsHitted` signal, closest boss-enemy identity, 0.45 pickup willingness threshold and On Your Own exclusion. When already fighting, personal native sight must have occurred within 2.5 seconds. A current visible shootable personal threat retains normal combat. These gates do not invent an unseen enemy or require a new SAIN patch.
- Against an already-known boss threat, Grunt tries available exact-target native fire, then safe stationary suppression using the same admitted native/fresh-report lanes as the explicit command. If neither is available, it tries a usable native cover position and then the native firing-position finder. Stationary support has a six-second attempt budget and a two-second actual-fire burst; it cannot start pursuit when the lane disappears. On Your Own also cancels an already-active boss-protection intent.
- Nearby push support reads Core push events or an addon automatic push with committed movement. It requires the same enemy and a pusher within 45 m directly / 65 m by complete route. Core's existing `IsTeamSearchSupportPosition` predicate is bound once: a native candidate may close toward the fight while remaining behind/beside the pusher, rather than becoming another assault. A point within 4 m of the pusher destination is rejected. No new push event system or Core sampler is added.
- Ordinary Grunt ally support takes an available exact-target shot before planning movement. Hidden automatic suppression is reserved for boss protection; ordinary ally/push support does not gain blind fire just because an initially visible target disappears.

### Position admission and execution

Grunt first considers at most four entries from SAIN's already-discovered cover list, skipping its occupied cover. Native `CoverAnalyzer.RecheckCoverPoint` validates cover and can update its position; all addon route, reservation and role checks therefore apply **after** recheck. A standing firing lane to native last-known geometry must also be clear. A selected cover position is executed as a committed support point using the existing support action; this does not install another native cover lifecycle. If no candidate qualifies, the shared native firing-position finder supplies one fallback candidate. Shooter continues using that finder directly.

Admission checks finite coordinates, floor, a complete route, reservations and meaningful displacement. Ordinary ally support cannot materially close on the enemy; Grunt push support instead uses the pusher-relative Core predicate, and boss support permits a useful firing approach. Routes remain capped at 45 m for Grunt / 90 m for Shooter, with Shooter retaining 16 m horizontal enemy standoff. Dependent followers remain inside their role's player regroup area. On Your Own removes that area constraint and disables boss protection while preserving teammate support and role/safety limits; explicit suppression still works independently.

Movement commits to that point using native walking. Four seconds without progress, a rejected path, changed knowledge/claim, a twenty-second attempt deadline, or two seconds at arrival without a shot ends it. A useful native shot during positional support returns control to normal combat; deliberately stationary Grunt supporting fire retains its bounded burst. Failed destinations are retained in a fixed four-point set with a four-second retry delay; the same failed point cannot continually rearm. Changed enemy/eight-metre knowledge shift or combat cleanup permits fresh planning. There is no broad Core geometry fallback or world-enemy scan.

## Search-party cooperation

The player remains the overall squad leader. GroupSearch retains the ready addon follower already searching the same living, known native enemy as a temporary search-party lead. An existing initiating Search stays a search; GroupSearch helpers cannot become leaders for other helpers. A valid assignment is retained across decision polls rather than reselected from squad enumeration order. Other native squad priorities and personal-contact gates remain unchanged.

SAINGrunt uses the adapted native FollowSearchParty action to follow that searcher, with native two-metre spacing, walking/running threshold and movement cadence. It never substitutes the player when the searcher disappears. Search completion, changed/forgotten enemy, medicine, death, tactic fallback or leaving the squad invalidates the assignment. The action releases only its own path on invalidation/handoff. Player commands and survival decisions keep their existing priority. On Your Own retains teammate cooperation and the existing independent combat rules.

SAINShooter treats a nearby searcher as an additional cue for the existing prepared ally-support objective, using the same 45 m direct / 65 m route helper range as push support. Settled-cover, medical, safety, player-area, 90 m route and 16 m enemy-standoff gates still apply. It avoids the searcher's current position and commits a native firing position before leaving its hold. The owned search-support intent ends when the assignment is lost. Outside that prepared-support entry, the existing Marksman GroupSearch translation still seeks a bounded firing position; a search-party cue cannot enable its proactive automatic-weapon assault branch. An already-owned movement or an explicit Need Sniper order retains its existing contract.

Selection scans only existing squad members at the normal squad decision boundary. Action updates validate the retained member with dictionary/identity/state checks; no new physics scan, world enumeration, patch or decision publisher is added. Shooter uses its existing shared native finder budget. Action reasons name the followed searcher; prepared support records source `sainSearch` through existing diagnostics.

## Cost, diagnostics and qualification

Opportunities are checked at most twice per second after admission gates. Native position search is spaced at least two seconds apart and shares its finder with the Marksman objective, so competing intents cannot double the synchronous native scan. Movement refresh and claim validation run at most once per second. Grunt cover reuse has a four-entry bound within the same two-second planning budget and creates no overlap query. The native finder retains its own 36-candidate bound; no SAIN search algorithm is changed.

Passive `sainSquadSupport` events and `objectives.squadSupport` snapshots expose mode (including `BossSupport` / `PushSupport`), source, stationary-fire intent, target, destination, ownership, completion reason and remaining attempt/burst time. `sainSuppressionFire` reports changes in the actual execution gate; snapshots retain its check time, native/admitted point, point source, direct/foliage lane result, missing fresh report, hard/friendly obstruction, running/low ammo, alignment wait, native trigger rejection or firing result. These fields are captured during execution; reading diagnostics performs no geometry, target selection or trigger checks. Recording never invokes decision or medical providers. [Validation](Validation.md) separates fixture/build evidence from pending Unity navigation, trigger behavior, mixed-squad usefulness and frame-time qualification. Broader friendly-fire work remains deferred.
